Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2025

Welcome to Chores Manager! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Account Data: Name, email address, or profile image (if sign-in is enabled).
  • User-Provided Data: Chore names, assignments, schedules, and notes.

b. Device Information

  • App Data: Anonymous info like device type, OS, and version for performance improvements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Deliver core functionality like chore tracking and reminders.
  • Sync data across devices (if enabled).
  • Fix bugs and enhance performance.
  • Analyze anonymized trends (if analytics are enabled).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your data. Limited sharing occurs only with trusted services for:

  • Authentication: e.g., Google/Apple sign-in (if enabled).
  • Cloud storage/backups: if you opt in.
  • Analytics: anonymized data to improve features.

4. Data Security

We use standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for cloud-stored data.
  • Secure HTTPS transmission.
  • Regular audits and security updates.

5. Your Choices

  • Decline to provide personal information.
  • Opt out of analytics (if available).
  • Delete your account/data via app or by request.

6. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without consent. Use in family settings should be under an adult’s account.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may change. You’ll be notified of significant updates via the app or email (if provided).
